Selected Research

EMNLP 2026 · SubmittedFirst Author

Combating Instruction Conflict via Energy-Driven Latent Conflict Detection

LLM Safety · Instruction Following · Representation Learning

We propose an energy-driven latent conflict detection framework, modeling system-constraint following as a low-energy equilibrium. Dual-view features built from the final hidden states and contrastive optimization create a robust energy decision barrier in latent space, turning conflict interception into a latent energy-distribution detection problem.
